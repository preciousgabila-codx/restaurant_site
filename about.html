Welcome to MAMA'S-KITECHEN home for authentic Cameroonian cuisine. Founded in 2012, we bring the vibrant energy and rich culinary heritage of Cameroon straight to your plate.

What began as Sunday eru and Khati-khati for family grew into
MAMA'S-KITCHEN. Every dish is still made with recipes passed down
